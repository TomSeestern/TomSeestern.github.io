## Classification Rationale
- **Decorative**: Icons, arrows, small badges that accompany text labels. These convey no independent information — the adjacent text or sr-only span provides the meaning. Set to alt="" so screen readers skip them.
- **Content**: Photos of people (hero, author), site logo (brand identifier). These convey information not available elsewhere in the DOM. Keep descriptive alt text.
