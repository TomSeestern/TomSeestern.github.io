| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Muted copy | `#6b7280` | `#9ca3af` | `muted` / `muted-dark` |
| Border | `#d1d5db` | `#4b5563` | `border` / `border-dark` |
| Accent | `#2563eb` | `#3b82f6` | `accent` / `accent-dark` |
| Soft badge | `#dbeafe` + `#1e40af` | `#1e3a8a` + `#93c5fd` | `accent-soft*` roles |

Dark foreground (`#ffffff`) over dark surface (`#111827`), muted copy (`#9ca3af`) over dark surface, accent (`#3b82f6`) over dark surface, and soft-badge foreground (`#93c5fd`) over soft dark badge (`#1e3a8a`) retain readable separation. Body now owns the semantic page surface, so transparent route fragments cannot expose an unintended light canvas.
