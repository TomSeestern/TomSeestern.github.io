were correct (`#site-nav` `display:none` closed at 375; contact submit 145×44,

  and `fe98d41`. `git merge-base --is-ancestor 272bb2b HEAD` = YES, but the
  positioning predates this refresh plan and was not introduced/changed by it.
  The plan's T21 scope was Flowbite removal, not toast repositioning.
