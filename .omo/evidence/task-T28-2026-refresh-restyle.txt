## D8 resolution — why "labelled examples only"

The original choice was *unlabelled* placeholder quotes, which violates AGENTS **R19.1**
(no claim presented as fact) and German **UWG §5** (misleading commercial practice).
Tom approved the documented alternative: placeholder quotes are permitted **only when
explicitly labelled as illustrative in the visible UI**. The decision record was updated
from BLOCKED to resolved (`Round 3`, `.omo/decisions/2026-09-10-refresh-scope-decisions.md`).
