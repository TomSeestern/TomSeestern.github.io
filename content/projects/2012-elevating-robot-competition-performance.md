After 2011 competition, we wanted to return better prepared. During 2012, I became team leader and principal developer. We worked with LEGO Mindstorms, LabVIEW, sensor kits, and a separate research presentation in PowerPoint.

First robot had exposed weak spots: design choices were rushed, testing was not systematic enough, and presentation needed same care as software. We recruited new members, raised extra funds for our kit, reworked robot design, and split research presentation into its own responsibility.

Year became long cycle of experiments, algorithm changes, sensor tests, team training, and rehearsals. Some ideas sounded good until robot met real competition setup. Repeating those tests taught me why preparation beats clever last-minute fixes.

Our placement improved. More importantly, I learned to lead a small group, turn broad goal into practical tasks, and give both technical work and presentation time they needed.
