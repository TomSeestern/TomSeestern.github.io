Altis Life grew from hobby modding into freelance work between 2014 and 2016. I led development and project management while still balancing school. Core stack was SQF, with C++ and Python also part of work.

We extended roleplay servers with stock markets, additional factions, and custom events. Each feature had to fit an economy and social system already in use by players. Server stability was constant concern once community servers reached more than 300 concurrent players.

Growth brought affiliate servers around world and freelance requests. I learned quickly that adding feature is easy part. Supporting it, debugging it under load, and explaining it to people running their own servers takes far more work.

This period gave me first experience of being responsible for software people depended on for their community. It made me more deliberate about testing changes, handling feedback, and finishing work instead of chasing next idea.
