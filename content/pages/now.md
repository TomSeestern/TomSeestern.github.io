# Now

I'm a senior developer working at an enterprise software company in Germany, building internal tooling and platform services. Most days involve TypeScript, Node.js, and the kind of infrastructure work that keeps a large monolith running while the team gradually carves out modern services.

Right now I'm deepening my Go knowledge. I've used it for smaller tools and CLI projects, but I want to reach the point where it's a comfortable first choice for backend work. The error handling model and the standard library are worth the learning curve.

This year I'm focused on two things: shipping a portfolio site I'm genuinely proud of, and finding my next role. I'm open to senior or staff-level positions, preferably remote or hybrid within Germany. If you're building something interesting with TypeScript, Go, or Rust, I'd like to hear about it.

Outside of work, I'm rebuilding my homelab for the fourth time. This iteration leans on NixOS and declarative configs. Fewer YOLO edits, more reproducibility. That's the theory, anyway.

Recent wins: shipped this site from scratch in a few focused sessions, migrated the homelab DNS setup to AdGuard Home, and finally understood generics in Go well enough to stop fighting the compiler.
